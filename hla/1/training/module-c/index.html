HLA 1 · Module C of 3

Sustaining School Culture

School culture is not something that is never ‘finished' for school leaders; it needs to be monitored and nurtured constantly. In Module C, we explore how to sustain school culture and even include the wider community in it.

Sessions in Module C

  1. D1Sustaining CultureParticipants reflect on the reality that culture is not something that ‘gets done’ - it needs constant maintenance, monitoring, and adjustment. In this session, participants review some ways that school culture can be weakened, and consider how to sustain the right culture.7 resources · Session Deck→
  2. D2Engaging Families in Building School CultureParticipants consider the role of families as critical stakeholders who must be included in school culture work. They reflect on the reasons why it can be hard to engage families in school life, and consider some new ways to engage families in building a supportive culture of high expectations.7 resources · Session Deck→
