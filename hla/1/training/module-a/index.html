HLA 1 · Module A of 3

Setting a Vision for School Culture

In Module A, we explore the importance of school culture, and the role that school leaders play in shaping school culture so that it is conducive for learning and growth. We look at the powerful role that teacher expectations play in learning, and explore how we might foster a culture of high expectations. We also consider how to ensure that the school culture is supportive, and we look at the critical role of relationships in building a supportive culture of high expectations. At the end of the Module, we think about how to engage teachers in co-creating the culture that we want to see.

Sessions in Module A

  1. E1Introduction to School CultureParticipants learn how beliefs, norms, and behavior shape school culture. They consider the culture of their own school, and explore culture with two contrasting case studies.9 resources · Session Deck→
  2. E2High ExpectationsParticipants learn about how teacher beliefs influence teacher behaviors and student outcomes, and consider how they may create routines, habits, and systems that reflect high expectations for all.8 resources · Session Deck→
  3. E3Creating a Supportive CultureParticipants reflect on the importance of a culture not only being one of high expectations - but one that is full of appropriate support, as well.10 resources · Session Deck→
  4. E4Relationships as the Heart of School CultureParticipants consider the critical role of relationships as the glue that holds a school together. They think of ways that they can build relationships with students and teachers.7 resources · Session Deck→
  5. E5Engaging Teachers with School CultureParticipants reflect on the school culture they would like to build, and consider how they can share that with teachers and include them in creating a supportive culture of high expectations.7 resources · Session Deck→
