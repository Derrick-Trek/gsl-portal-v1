Job-embedded Assignments for School Leaders

The following section presents job-embedded assignment options for school leaders. Each assignment aligns with a specific level within the HLA, thus allowing school leaders the opportunity to isolate and practice specific skills. The ‘task’ outlines the main activity to help school leaders practice a specific skill. The ‘next steps’ outline follow-up activities the school leader can do to continue the practice in their school.

Each assignment also provides guidance for coaches supporting school leaders through this practice. Please refer to the box labeled ‘for coaches’ at the end of each assignment to understand the links with HLA levels and access a list of success indicators to help review the completed assignment.

The ‘share it’ section is intended to help school leaders connect with and learn from their peers by having them share their plans, reflections, and progress with the rest of their cohort.

  1. SL Assignment 1 Google Docs ↗To help school leaders practice developing trust and rapport with staff.
  2. SL Assignment 2 Google Docs ↗To help school leaders practice recognizing people’s efforts and appreciating them for it.
  3. SL Assignment 3 Google Docs ↗To help school leaders practice engaging staff and students in building school culture.
  4. SL Assignment 4 Google Docs ↗To help school leaders practice reflecting on school culture and engaging staff members in the culture-building process.
  5. SL Assignment 5 Google Docs ↗To help you practice monitoring your school culture through different methods.
  6. SL Assignment 6 Google Docs ↗To help school leaders practice engaging families in school culture.

Job-embedded Assignments for Teachers

The following section presents job-embedded assignment options for teachers. Each assignment aligns with a specific level within the HLA, thus allowing teachers the opportunity to isolate and practice specific skills. The ‘task’ outlines the main activity to help teachers practice a specific skill. The ‘next steps’ outline follow-up activities the teacher can do to continue the practice in their classroom.

Each assignment also provides guidance for school leaders/coaches supporting teachers through this practice. Please refer to the box labeled ‘for coaches’ at the end of each assignment to understand the links with HLA levels and access a list of success indicators to help review the completed assignment.

The ‘share it’ section is intended to help teachers connect with and learn from their peers by having them share their plans, reflections, and progress with the rest of the team.

  1. Teacher Assignment 1 Google Docs ↗To help teachers practice strengthening their relationship with students.
  2. Teacher Assignment 2 Google Docs ↗To help teachers practice building simple classroom routines that can help save instructional time.
  3. Teacher Assignment 3 Google Docs ↗To help teachers practice reflecting on and discussing appropriate behaviors with students.
  4. Teacher Assignment 4 Google Docs ↗To help teachers practice discussing, encouraging, and correcting classroom behaviors with students.
  5. Teacher Assignment 5 Google Docs ↗To help teachers practice engaging families in supporting student behavior in the classroom.
  6. Teacher Assignment 6 Google Docs ↗To help teachers practice engaging families in discussions on a supportive culture of high expectations.
