Learn About HLAs

Improving school leadership is an essential part of ensuring that all children receive an excellent education. But school leadership is so complex that it is hard to know what to prioritize since there are so many things that could affect teaching and learning! After reviewing the literature on school leadership, consulting with experts, and working with practitioners and school leaders, we have identified six high leverage actions (HLAs) for school leaders. These actions are intricately interconnected, forming a cohesive framework for effective school leadership.
